Inside Ultra Luxury

Why members choose Ultra Luxury

  • Exclusive Panatta experience

    Train on Italian-engineered Panatta equipment, trusted worldwide for biomechanics, performance and precision.

  • Advanced recovery

    From cryotherapy and oxygen therapy to steam rooms, spa experiences and recovery lounges.

  • Personalised coaching

    Every programme is tailored to your body, goals and lifestyle, with measurable progress and expert support.

  • Limited membership

    Aurum intentionally keeps memberships limited, creating a more private and personal environment.

Recovery suite

  • Cryotherapy

    Rapid cold exposure to reduce inflammation and accelerate muscle recovery.

  • Ice Plunge

    Cold immersion to sharpen circulation, resilience and post-training recovery.

  • Red Light Therapy

    Targeted light therapy supporting cellular repair, skin health and faster healing.

  • Infrared Sauna

    Deep heat that penetrates muscles, relieves tension and supports circulation.

  • Steam Room

    Moist heat to relax muscles, open airways and clear the mind.

  • Spa Therapies

    Restorative treatments for body and mind, designed around recovery.

  • Oxygen Therapy

    Enriched oxygen sessions to boost energy, focus and recovery capacity.

  • Hydrotherapy

    Water-based therapy to ease joints, aid mobility and calm the body.

  • Vichy Shower

    A signature cascading water therapy for full-body renewal.

Frequently asked questions

What makes Aurum one of the best luxury gyms in Bangalore?

Aurum combines premium facilities, Italian Panatta equipment, personalised coaching, advanced recovery therapies and a limited membership model for a truly exclusive fitness experience.

Can I visit Aurum before becoming a member?

Yes. We recommend booking a private club tour to explore the facilities, meet our team and understand the membership experience.

Do you offer personal training?

Yes. Every personal training programme is customised to your goals, fitness level and lifestyle.

Is Aurum suitable for beginners?

Absolutely. Whether you are new to fitness or an experienced athlete, our coaches build structured programmes around your journey.

What wellness facilities are included?

Members can access recovery therapies, premium changing rooms, steam, spa services, oxygen therapy, cryotherapy and more, depending on the selected membership.
