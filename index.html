What types of memberships are offered?

We offer day passes and monthly, quarterly, half-yearly and annual memberships, along with special rates for students and seniors.

What are the gym's hours?

We open early and close late to fit your busy lifestyle: Monday to Saturday, 5:30 AM to 11:00 PM, and Sunday, 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM.

Is a fitness assessment required?

No, but we recommend a free assessment for new members so we can tailor your workout plan.

What safety protocols are in place?

We ensure rigorous cleaning and provide hand sanitiser throughout every club.

Personal training can be booked at the front desk, by phone, or online through the form below.
